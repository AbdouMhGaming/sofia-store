# PART 8 — INVENTORY AND DATABASE BUSINESS RULES

Design the database around product variants.

A product is not the same as a product variant.

Example:

Product: Elegant Black Dress

Variants:

- Black / Size M / Barcode 100001
- Black / Size L / Barcode 100002
- Black / Size XL / Barcode 100003

Each variant must have its own SKU and stock balance.

Required database entities:

- users
- roles

- customers
- customer_addresses
- categories
- brands
- products
- product_images
- sizes
- colors
- product_variants
- locations
- inventory
- stock_movements
- suppliers
- stock_receipts
- stock_receipt_items
- sales
- sale_items
- payments
- refunds
- refund_items
- register_sessions
- orders
- order_items
- stock_reservations
- deliveries
- order_status_history
- audit_logs
- store_settings

You can add other tables when necessary.

Important inventory rules:

Available stock = quantity on hand - quantity reserved.

Online orders and physical-store sales must use the same inventory system.

Use database transactions and appropriate row locking or atomic conditional updates to prevent overselling.

Stock must never become negative unless a specifically authorized adjustment workflow permits it.

Record every stock movement.

Historical sale prices must remain unchanged when product prices are edited.

Use proper decimal or integer currency representations. Never use floating-point arithmetic for financial calculations.
